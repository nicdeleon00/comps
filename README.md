# Comps

Pokémon card prices, collection tracking, trading and nearby stock, on the web and on iPhone.

- **Website:** https://nicdeleon00.github.io/comps/
- **iPhone app:** [Comps.ipa](https://github.com/nicdeleon00/comps/releases/download/ios-latest/Comps.ipa) (install with Sideloadly, AltStore or SideStore)

What it does:

- **Collection** — scan cards (one at a time, a stack, or a binder page) and see what your collection is worth.
- **Trade** — trade with other collectors: offers with cards and cash, counters and messages.
- **Deals** — cards priced under what they're worth.
- **Stock** — which nearby stores have Pokémon cards in stock.
- **Master Sets** — every card in every printing, and what's left to complete a set.

One account works on the website and in the app.
